How we approach ppc advertising

Start with a clear objective, understand the audience and select activities that fit your resources. The exact plan should be agreed before work begins.

  • Campaign structure: Organize campaigns around services, audience intent and goals.
  • Keyword research: Identify relevant searches and add negatives to reduce waste.
  • Ad messaging: Write accurate ads that match the landing-page offer.
  • Conversion tracking: Configure measurement for meaningful actions before judging results.

Planning and measurement

Define the goal, confirm tracking where appropriate, establish a baseline and review results regularly. Campaign performance depends on the offer, competition, budget, audience and execution.

What we need from you

Useful inputs include your business goals, audience, key products or services, available budget, brand guidelines and any existing performance data you can share safely.

Service scope, pricing and availability should be confirmed directly. No specific results are guaranteed.
