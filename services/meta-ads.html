How we approach meta & social media advertising

Start with a clear objective, understand the audience and select activities that fit your resources. The exact plan should be agreed before work begins.

  • Campaign objective: Select an objective that aligns with the intended customer action.
  • Creative testing: Test variations in messaging and visuals in a controlled way.
  • Audience planning: Choose relevant targeting and placements while respecting platform policies.
  • Performance review: Review spend, conversions and other relevant metrics before making changes.

Planning and measurement

Define the goal, confirm tracking where appropriate, establish a baseline and review results regularly. Campaign performance depends on the offer, competition, budget, audience and execution.

What we need from you
